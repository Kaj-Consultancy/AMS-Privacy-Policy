Privacy Policy

Index AMS — Attendance Management System

Effective date: 6 October 2026  ·  Applies to the Index AMS mobile app (iOS and Android) and the Index AMS web portal, published by Index Solution.

1. Who this policy is for

Index AMS is a workplace attendance system. You normally use it because your employer has enrolled you in it. Your employer decides what information is held about you and who inside the organisation can see it; Index Solution operates the system on your employer's behalf.

The app is built for working adults and is not directed at children.

2. Information we collect

a. Account information, provided by your employer

  • Name
  • Email address, which is also your sign-in name
  • Phone number, where your employer records one
  • Your role and the team, department or site you belong to
  • Your work schedule

b. Attendance information

  • Check-in and check-out times
  • Leave requests, attendance corrections and overtime, together with who approved or declined them
  • Remarks you type into a request

c. Location

When you check in or check out, the app reads your device's precise location once and sends it with that single attendance record, so your employer can confirm the mark was made at the assigned site.

The app does not follow you. Location is read only at the moment you tap check in or check out, and only while the app is open on your screen. There is no background tracking, no location history between marks, and no location is read when you are not marking attendance.

d. Device information

  • A notification token issued by Apple or Google, used only to deliver the app's own notifications to your device
  • Device model and operating system version, and basic diagnostic information used to find and fix faults

e. Photo library

The app asks for permission to save to your photo library only when you choose to export or share a report from your device. It does not read, scan or upload your existing photos.

f. What we do not collect

The app does not use your camera, microphone, contacts, calendar, call logs or messages, and does not read files on your device.

3. How the information is used

InformationWhat it is used for
Name, email, roleSigning you in, and showing the right screens for your role
Attendance timesRecording attendance and producing your employer's attendance reports
LocationConfirming an attendance mark was made at the assigned site
Notification tokenSending you app notifications, such as a decision on your request

None of this information is used for advertising, and none of it is used to build a profile of you across other companies' apps or websites.

4. Tracking and advertising

Index AMS does not track you. We do not use advertising identifiers, we do not share your information with data brokers or advertising networks, and we do not combine your information with data from other companies for advertising purposes.

5. Who can see your information

  • Your employer. Administrators and the managers responsible for you can see your attendance and your requests, as their role allows.
  • Nobody at another company. Each organisation's data is kept separate. One employer's users cannot reach another employer's records.
  • Service providers who host the system and deliver notifications and email on our behalf, under contract and only for those purposes.
  • Where the law requires it, or where it is necessary to protect the safety and security of users or of the system.

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal information.

6. Storage and security

Information is held on managed servers. Traffic between the app and our servers is encrypted in transit, passwords are stored hashed and never in readable form, and access is restricted by role. No system can promise perfect security, but we take reasonable measures to protect your information and review them as the system changes.

7. How long it is kept

Attendance records are kept for as long as your employer needs them as an employment record, and for as long as the law requires. Diagnostic information is kept only for a short period. When your employer's use of the service ends, their data is removed or returned according to their agreement with us.

8. Your choices and rights

  • Location permission can be refused or withdrawn at any time in your device settings. Attendance marking from the app needs it, so without it you would mark attendance another way your employer provides.
  • Notifications can be turned off in your device settings at any time.
  • Access, correction and deletion. Because your records belong to your employer, please ask your employer's administrator first. You may also write to us at the address below and we will work with your employer to answer you.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app changes. The effective date at the top always shows when it was last revised, and the current version is always published at this address.
